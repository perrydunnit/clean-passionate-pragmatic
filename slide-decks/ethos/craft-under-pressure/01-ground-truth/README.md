# Ground Truth

*The gap between the map and the ground.* Session 1 of 3 in [Craft Under Pressure](../).

## Recommended prerequisites

None. This is the first session, and the opening slide assumes nothing about which session you were in last — or whether you were in one at all. It asks about the week, not about a previous topic.

No prior reading is required either (see below).

## What this session does

Gives the room a word for what is happening that is neither "I'm burned out" nor "my manager is lying". Nothing more. The other two sessions assume this one landed.

The claim, in three parts:

1. The exhaustion people call burnout is often something else, and rest is not the remedy for it.
2. The tension belongs to the role — a role is a set of expectations, and legitimate expectations contradict each other by design (Katz & Kahn, *The Social Psychology of Organizations*, ch. 7).
3. A group resolves that tension by agreeing on a story, which is not the same as lying (Festinger, *A Theory of Cognitive Dissonance*, ch. 1 and 4).

**The relief it delivers:** it is not a personal failing, and no holiday is going to fix it.

## Reading Assignment

**Attendees: nothing.** This is the only session in the series with no reading, and it is deliberate. Handing colleagues a reading list about their own moral distress turns the first session into homework about their problems, and the people who most need to be there are the least likely to do it. The deck supplies the vocabulary instead.

**Presenters:** Festinger, ch. 1 and 4, and Katz & Kahn, ch. 7 as background. Katz & Kahn is several hundred pages of 1960s academic prose and is a reference work, not a read-through.

## The Practice

**The Audit** — fifteen minutes, one page, two columns.

- *Column one:* what the document says. Its words, copied verbatim.
- *Column two:* what you know to be true. Your words.

Eight minutes alone, then pairs compare the columns rather than the people. Nothing is collected and nothing is shared with the group. This is a diagnostic, not an action, which is the right shape for the first session of three.
